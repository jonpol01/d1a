### Changed

- torch 2.14 (#355): `torch>=2.6,<2.15`, locked at 2.14.1 (torchvision 0.29.1). Its MPS embedding kernel reads rows past
  2^31 elements correctly (pytorch/pytorch#188756), so Gemma 4's per-layer embeddings are right on Apple GPUs without
  d1a's guard (#354), which stays for older torch.
