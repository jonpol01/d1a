# Routing labels (v1)

Synthetic prompts for training D1A to choose a model tier: small, medium, or large.

## Tiers

Label is the **smallest** tier that still answers the request well.

- **small** (2-4B local model): common fact lookup, translate/reword/fix grammar of a short text, change politeness of one sentence, format/sort/convert, one-line summary of a short text, trivial yes/no or sentiment, simple definition, greeting.
- **medium** (9-30B): ordinary email, letter, report section or meeting notes; routine function, SQL, regex, unit test or small script (write/explain/fix); explain a known concept with examples; extract fields to JSON; summarize a long document into action items; compare 2-3 options; translate a long business document.
- **large** (frontier): root-cause a hard bug/outage/performance problem from logs; design a system/architecture/data model; plan a large migration or refactor; security, threat-model or legal/contract risk review; proof or derivation; multi-step analysis across data or several documents; optimise complex concurrent/GPU code; strategy with many trade-offs.

## Hard cases

About 20% of each tier. These are prompts a length-or-keyword heuristic would mis-route:

- long-but-easy (long pasted text, trivial request) → still **small**
- short-but-hard (one line that needs deep reasoning) → **large** (or **medium** if the work is ordinary coding/writing)
- ordinary business writing (an email or status update looks short) → **medium**, not small
- scary words but easy (e.g. "what does 'race condition' mean?") → **small**

## Line format

One JSON object per line in `routing-v1.jsonl`:

`{"id": "r0001", "prompt": "...", "tier": "small|medium|large", "lang": "en|ja", "category": "<snake_case>", "hard_case": true|false}`

Ids are `r0001` upward. Attachments appear as placeholders such as `[log follows]` or `[契約書を添付]`. No real names, emails, phone numbers, customer data, or secrets.
